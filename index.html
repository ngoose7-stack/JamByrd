How to record & jam with friends

Laying down a track

  1. Plug in wired headphones (Lightning/USB-C or an adapter) so the mic doesn't pick up the other tracks. AirPods add delay and lower mic quality while recording.
  2. Tap R on a track to arm it (allow the mic the first time).
  3. Tap the Tempo bar to set the speed (use Tap tempo or the +/− buttons), then hit ●. You get a count-in, then it records while you hear everything else.
  4. Hit ▶/■ or ● again to stop. Not happy? Record again; the take is replaced (or use Punch-in mode in Settings to redo just one part).

Friends in the room

  • Give each person a track and optionally tap Player to add their name
  • Pass the phone (or plug in a USB audio interface and pick it under Settings → Input). Arm their track and record.
  • Use M (mute) and S (solo) to check parts, and the sliders to balance the mix.

Friends somewhere else

  1. Tap Share and send the link. Every song is saved online automatically once something is recorded.
  2. Your friend opens the link and sees the same song with everything already recorded. They tap + Add track (or R on an empty track) and record their layer.
  3. New takes show up live for everyone who has the song open (the red “● 2 here” shows who's in). Find all your songs under ⋯ → My songs.

If takes sound late or early

Open Settings → Calibrate once on each phone (headphones off, volume up). Avoid Bluetooth headphones while recording; they add a lot of delay.
